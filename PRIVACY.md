# CoinTex Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

CoinTex is a game by Ahmed Fawzy Gad. It does not require an account and does
not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. CoinTex does
not sell personal information.

## Information stored on your device

CoinTex stores game progress and settings in the app's private storage on your
device. This information is not sent to the developer. You can clear it with
the **Reset progress** option in the game, or remove it by uninstalling the app.

## Multiplayer

Multiplayer is optional. When you choose to host or join a game, CoinTex makes
a direct connection between the two players. Gameplay messages, such as player
movement and game state, are sent to the other player for the duration of that
session. CoinTex does not route or retain these messages on a developer server.

Opening the **Host Game** screen asks `api.ipify.org` for your public IP address
over an encrypted HTTPS connection so the app can show the address needed for
optional internet play. The request necessarily reveals your public IP address
to that service. The ipify service states that it does not log visitor
information. CoinTex does not retain the returned public IP address after you
leave the host screen.

On Apple platforms, CoinTex requests Local Network access for nearby
multiplayer. It does not request your precise location, contacts, photos,
camera, or microphone.

## Third-party platform services

Apple, Google, and the store or operating-system services you use may process
download, payment, diagnostics, or crash information under their own privacy
policies. CoinTex does not receive that information unless you separately send
it when requesting support.

## Children's privacy

CoinTex is not designed to collect personal information from children or any
other players. If you believe information was provided to the developer in
error, contact the address below.
